Description

A centerpiece of refinement, this 19th-century dressing table features harmonious curved lines and an integrated oval mirror, exuding classic charm. With a rich wood finish and ornate details, this table is ideal for those seeking to combine historical functionality with a timeless luxury aesthetic.

History & Provenance

Historical Context

Typical of mid-19th-century cabinetmaking, this dressing table was an essential fixture in the boudoir of elite households. It served not only a functional purpose but also as a demonstration of the owner's status and taste for ornate, craftsmanship-heavy furniture.

Provenance

Estate of a French aristocratic family, 1860-1930;
Auctioned in Lyon, 1955;
Antique collection, Brussels, 1970-2005;
Acquired by current owner, 2010.
