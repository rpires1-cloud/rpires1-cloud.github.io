How to Participate

Important guidelines and rules for bidding at HÉRITAGE auctions

Registration

All bidders must register at least 48 hours before the auction. Valid identification and proof of funds may be required.

Buyer’s Premium

A buyer’s premium of 25% is added to the hammer price. This includes insurance and handling.

Payment Terms

Payment is due within 7 business days of the auction. We accept bank transfers and major cards.

Bidding Increments

Bidding increments vary by lot value. Typically €100 for lots under €5,000 and higher for premium lots.

Absentee

Cannot attend in person? Submit absentee bids or arrange phone bidding by contacting us in advance.

Viewing Schedule

All lots are available for viewing 3 days prior to auction during business hours.
