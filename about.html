Our History

Where history meets elegance

Since 1982, Héritage has been a sanctuary for the world's finest antiques and decorative arts. We believe that true luxury lies not in the new, but in the enduring beauty of objects that have witnessed history unfold. Today, HÉRITAGE continues this legacy with the same dedication to excellence that defined our founding. Our expert team travels across Europe and beyond, meticulously selecting pieces that embody exceptional craftsmanship, provenance, and artistic merit. From Baroque grandeur to Art Nouveau fluidity, each acquisition represents a chapter in the ongoing narrative of human creativity.

Our values

The principles that guide everything we do

Every piece is meticulously verified for provenance and authenticity by our expert team.

Our love for history and craftsmanship drives everything we do, from acquisition to curation.

We uphold the highest ethical standards in all our dealings, ensuring trust and transparency.

We preserve cultural legacies, connecting the past with collectors who appreciate timeless beauty.

Our journey

Over four decades, Héritage has grown from a single gallery to a cornerstone of European antique curation. Each milestone represents our commitment to preserving cultural heritage and serving discerning collectors worldwide.

1982

Héritage founded in Lisbon by Isabella Monteiro

1995

Expanded to include European auction services

2005

Opened dedicated restoration workshop

2012

Established partnerships with major European museums

2020

Launched digital catalogue and virtual viewings

2026

Celebrating over 40 years of curatorial excellence
